# A deeper topic

This is the deepest page in the demo. The sidebar still shows the full path to it.

## Why nest pages

Nesting keeps related topics together. Use it when a topic has several parts that readers will visit in order.

## Keep it shallow

Even though the layout handles any depth, two or three levels are usually enough. Deeper trees are harder to scan.

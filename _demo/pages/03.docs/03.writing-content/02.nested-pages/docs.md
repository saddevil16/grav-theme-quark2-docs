# Nested pages

The sidebar mirrors your folder structure, so topics can be nested as deep as you like.

## How nesting looks

The section you are reading is open in the sidebar, and its parents are open too. Collapsed sections show a small arrow that expands them.

## Try it

Open the pages below to see the sidebar follow you down the tree:

- [A subtopic](/docs/writing-content/nested-pages/a-subtopic)
- [A deeper topic](/docs/writing-content/nested-pages/a-subtopic/a-deeper-topic)

## Remembered state

Sections you open by hand stay open while you browse. The theme stores this for the browser session only, and you can turn it off with the **Remember expanded sidebar sections** option.
